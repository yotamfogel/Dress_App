Casual – relaxed, everyday wear (jeans, t‑shirts, hoodies, sneakers).

Classy / Elegant – polished, refined outfits (tailored pieces, silk blouses, pencil skirts).

Business / Office Wear – formal or smart work attire (blazers, dress pants, button‑ups).

Business Casual – a blend of professional and relaxed (chinos, loafers, cardigans).

Streetwear – urban, trend‑driven (graphic tees, oversized hoodies, sneakers, caps).

Athleisure – sporty and comfortable, worn casually (leggings, joggers, track jackets).

Bohemian (Boho) – free‑spirited, flowy (maxi dresses, crochet, fringe, earthy tones).

Minimalist – clean lines, neutral palettes (simple cuts, monochrome outfits).

Preppy – classic and collegiate (polo shirts, pleated skirts, loafers).

Grunge – edgy, 90s inspired (flannels, ripped jeans, band tees, combat boots).

Vintage / Retro – inspired by past decades (50s dresses, 70s flares, 90s tracksuits).

Y2K – early 2000s vibe (baby tees, low-rise jeans, sparkly accessories).

Edgy / Punk – rebellious aesthetic (leather jackets, studs, plaid pants).

Goth – dark and dramatic (black lace, corsets, heavy boots).

Chic – effortlessly stylish (trendy cuts, tailored staples, high‑quality fabrics).

Romantic – soft and feminine (ruffles, pastels, floral prints).

Cottagecore – whimsical, countryside inspired (flowy dresses, puff sleeves, aprons).

Artsy / Eclectic – bold, unique combinations (colorful prints, unusual silhouettes).

Avant‑Garde – experimental and fashion‑forward (architectural shapes, asymmetry).

Resort / Cruise Wear – vacation ready (linen sets, kaftans, straw hats).

Evening / Formal Wear – gowns, tuxedos, cocktail dresses.
